2014Learn the process

Before automating work, understand where it actually breaks.

Process improvement

Operational work became a systems problem.

Research, Lean projects, logistics, banking, data entry, and workflow modelling built the habit of following work across people, tools, and exceptions.

What this unlocked

The ability to recognise that local optimisation is often useless when information, ownership, and decisions do not travel through the whole process.

2020Make delivery repeatable

Spreadsheets became tools. Projects became delivery patterns.

Data migration

Source data needed more than another dashboard.

SAP migrations and multi-country delivery exposed the same recurring problem: messy inputs, implicit rules, brittle transformations, and too little evidence about what changed.

What this unlocked

Reusable templates, field analysis, validation logic, and a stronger separation between source truth, transformation, and output.
